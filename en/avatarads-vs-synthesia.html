What Synthesia is

Synthesia is an AI video creation platform that turns a text script into a video with a realistic talking human avatar. Based in London, it’s one of the world leaders in the field, focused on businesses: training and L&D, internal communications, multilingual videos. You type a script, pick a “stock” avatar (125 to 240+ depending on the plan), and the tool generates a presenter-in-front-of-slides video, with synthetic voices in 160+ languages, automatic dubbing (AI Dubbing), templates, branding, SCORM export and interactive videos on higher tiers.

Synthesia’s strengths

  • An established, credible leader in the AI avatar market, with strong trust from enterprises (large corporations).
  • Talking avatar realism (talking-head) among the best on the market.
  • Huge output language coverage: 140–160+ languages and voices, powerful automatic dubbing (AI Dubbing).
  • Built-in voice cloning and personal avatars — useful for producing at scale in several languages.
  • A mature professional ecosystem: SCORM export, interactive videos, collaboration, SSO, API — designed for training and communications.
  • A free plan (10 min/month) to try it with no card.

If your main need is multilingual training or corporate video with an avatar presenter, Synthesia is an excellent choice.

Why choose AvatarAds

AvatarAds doesn’t play on the same field: it targets “faceless” ads and social UGC. Its real differentiators compared with Synthesia:

  • Ads / UGC positioning: Express (influencer-style UGC via Veo), Motion Control (1080p), avatar + hooks — closer to social content and creatives than to a corporate avatar presenter.
  • An all-in-one suite beyond the talking avatar: AI Images (4K product ads), AI Editing (raw audio → edited video), audio cleanup, a CapCut-style editor — a range Synthesia doesn’t cover.
  • Official MCP connector for Claude: generate and drive creation from a conversation — Synthesia has no equivalent.
  • Your real voice, recorded or uploaded + animated captions, with no forced voice cloning.
  • Prices in euros, no commitment (Whop): Starter €29.99, Pro €49.99, Elite from €89.99, −25% on yearly billing.
  • Built in France by IA Manager, a French company.

To be honest: entry prices are close (€29.99 vs $29), and Synthesia keeps the edge on the size of its avatar library and on voice cloning. AvatarAds’ advantage lies in the Claude connector, UGC and the all-in-one ad suite.

Frequently asked questions

It depends on your goal. Choose Synthesia for business video: an avatar presenter reading a script, training (L&D), internal communications, multilingual dubbing and a huge avatar library. Choose AvatarAds for ads and “faceless” UGC on social media: your own voice with no forced cloning, an MCP connector for Claude and an all-in-one suite (Express UGC, Motion Control, 4K AI Images, AI Editing, editor).
How much do AvatarAds and Synthesia cost?
Synthesia is billed in dollars: Free plan at $0/month (10 min, watermarked videos), Starter at $29/month ($18 billed yearly) and Creator at $89/month ($64 billed yearly), Enterprise on quote. Does AvatarAds clone my voice like Synthesia?
No, and that’s a deliberate choice. AvatarAds uses your real voice, recorded or uploaded, combined with animated captions. Synthesia relies on built-in speech synthesis and voice cloning — a real strength for multilingual production, but a different approach from AvatarAds’, which focuses on the authenticity of your real voice.
Which languages does Synthesia support?
Synthesia produces voices, captions and dubbing in 160+ output languages. Its interface itself is mainly in English.
