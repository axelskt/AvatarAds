What Arcads (Arcads AI) is

Arcads is an American platform that turns a text script into a UGC-style video ad, performed by a realistic “AI actor”. It’s designed for performance marketers who want to test lots of creatives quickly.

  • A library of AI actors (the website shows “1000+”, some reviews mention ~300), filterable by gender, age and setting.
  • Script → video in about 2 minutes, with control over emotions and expressions through the text.
  • Multilingual output (30+ languages) and speech-to-speech voice cloning for brand consistency.
  • Batch generation to produce dozens of variants for A/B testing.
  • “One-click” editing (B-roll, music, captions, transitions) and a recent video model (Seedance 2.5).

Worth noting: billing is in dollars, and there’s no free plan or free trial — the price only appears after you create an account.

Arcads (Arcads AI)’s strengths

To be fair: on its home turf, Arcads is an excellent tool.

  • Very realistic UGC output: the talking-head AI actors are often hard to tell apart from a real creator — a recurring strength in user feedback.
  • A very large library of AI actors and presets built for performance marketing.
  • Voice cloning (speech-to-speech): a real asset of Arcads for brand consistency, which AvatarAds deliberately doesn’t offer.
  • Speed and volume: script → video in ~2 min and batch generation, ideal for testing lots of hooks.
  • 30+ output languages and “consenting” actors (an ethical argument they put forward).
  • Mature positioning in creative testing for D2C brands, mobile apps and agencies.
