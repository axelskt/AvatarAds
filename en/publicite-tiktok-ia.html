Why generate your TikTok ads with AI

On TikTok, performance comes from the number of creatives you test. Generating your ads with AI removes the cost and delays of a shoot: no actor, no studio, no gear needed. You produce credible UGC-style videos at scale, for less, and you stay in control of every hook. AvatarAds brings these tools together in a single interface, published by IA Manager (France) and designed for creators, e-commerce sellers, small businesses and agencies.

Create a 9:16 UGC-style TikTok ad in minutes

  • Express (Veo engine): describe the scene in text or drop in an image, choose a UGC style, and generate a vertical video ready to publish.
  • Generator: an AI avatar speaks with your real voice (recorded or uploaded) and animated captions — no voice cloning.
  • Motion Control: animate a photo (product, character) based on the motion of a reference video, in native 1080p.

Videos come out in 9:16 vertical format, ready for TikTok right from generation.

Hooks and variants: test several hooks with A/B testing

The foundation of a high-performing creative strategy is testing lots of hooks. AvatarAds lets you quickly spin up several variants of the same ad (hooks, angles) to find what converts. You generate, compare and iterate without going back to a shoot — the key to scaling TikTok and Meta ads.

Animated captions and your real voice for an authentic result

The Generator pairs your real voice, recorded or uploaded, with synced animated captions — an authentic result, with none of the risks of voice cloning. It’s a deliberate choice by AvatarAds: your voice rather than a clone. If you prefer, Express can also produce a generated voice.

AI Images and 4K product ads for e-commerce

For DTC and e-commerce creatives, the AI Images module generates visuals and product ads in 4K, and Motion Control animates a product image based on a reference video. You get credible product creatives with no photo shoot, to combine with your UGC videos.

Publish on TikTok, Instagram Reels and Shorts with the same creative

The 9:16 vertical format works directly on TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts: a single creative can be published on all three platforms. You can fine-tune the result in the built-in CapCut-style editor and clean up the sound with the audio cleanup module before publishing.
