What Creatify is

Creatify is an American AI video ad platform, focused on e-commerce and performance ads. Its flagship promise: turn a product link (or an image, or a brief) into a complete video ad — script, AI avatar, voice-over, b-roll, captions — in minutes, via its URL-to-Video feature.

  • 1500+ “ultra-realistic” AI avatars, lip-sync announced at 24 fps, 75+ languages and 140+ voices in output.
  • “Bring Your Own Avatar” (avatar cloning from your own video) and voice cloning with emotional control.
  • Batch Mode to generate several variants at once, Creative Agent (a conversational copilot) and Performance Agent (ad performance analysis + competitor ad tracker).
  • Ad Cloner, Product Ads (product visuals/animations), the Ad Flow node editor and a public API.

Its prices are in dollars: Free plan (10 credits/month, watermarked export), Starter $39/month (100 credits, no watermark), Pro $99/month (the most popular, 1500+ avatars + custom avatars, Performance Agent) and Enterprise on quote. Worth noting: unused credits don’t roll over from one month to the next.

Creatify’s strengths

To be fair — these are real assets, some of which AvatarAds doesn’t aim to cover:

  • A huge avatar library (1500+), filterable and ready to use.
  • Avatar AND voice cloning built in — useful if you want a digital twin or to reproduce a specific voice (an approach AvatarAds deliberately doesn’t offer).
  • Native “product link → ad” automation for e-commerce, hard to match for quickly producing creatives from a product page.
  • A performance layer: competitor ad tracking and performance analysis, rare among competitors.
  • Batch Mode + a robust API to test dozens of variants and industrialise the workflow.
  • A free plan to try before paying.
