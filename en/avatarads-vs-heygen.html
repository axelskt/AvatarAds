AvatarAds vs HeyGen: which one should you choose?

Short answer: HeyGen wins on lip-sync dubbing (175+ languages), voice cloning and the avatar catalogue; AvatarAds wins on using your real voice, the MCP connector for Claude and the all-in-one advertising suite. Both start at a comparable price (≈ €29/$29 a month).

Both tools create AI videos, but they don’t serve the same purpose. HeyGen is a leader in photorealistic avatars: it excels at lip-sync dubbing in 175+ languages, voice cloning and corporate or training video at an international scale. AvatarAds is built for ads and UGC: your real voice (no cloning) with animated captions, an MCP connector for Claude and an all-in-one suite (Express, Motion Control, 4K AI Images, AI Editing). If you want multilingual dubbing and a large avatar library, HeyGen; if you want to produce ad creatives without showing your face, AvatarAds.

What HeyGen is

HeyGen is an American AI video generation platform and a market leader in avatars. It creates photorealistic talking avatars (a “digital twin” from a clip of about 15 seconds), with identity consistency, micro-expressions and lip-sync. Its centrepiece is lip-sync dubbing and translation in 175+ languages, combined with voice cloning.

It offers 700+ stock avatars, custom avatars (unlimited from the Creator plan), a “Video Agent” that produces the script, visuals, voice-over and video from a prompt, an AI Studio editor, text-to-video, image-to-video, audio-to-video and PDF-to-video building blocks, interactive video on higher plans, plus an API and enterprise features. Pricing (in dollars): Free at $0, Creator at $29/month, Pro at $49/month, Business at $149 + $20/seat, and Enterprise on quote.

HeyGen’s strengths

To be fair: on its home turf, HeyGen is very strong.

  • Avatar realism among the best on the market, with good identity consistency on long videos.
  • Lip-sync dubbing and translation in 175+ languages — an industry reference for localisation.
  • Native voice cloning, which AvatarAds deliberately doesn’t offer.
  • 700+ stock avatars, ready to use.
  • Mature Video Agent, API and enterprise features; strong adoption (HeyGen claims 85% of the Fortune 100).
  • A free plan to try it out (with a watermark).

Worth noting: HeyGen does have an editor, image-to-video and audio-to-video. It would be wrong to claim it “only” does avatars.

Why choose AvatarAds

AvatarAds isn’t trying to beat HeyGen on multilingual dubbing or cloning. Its advantage lies elsewhere, on advertising:

  • Official MCP connector for the Claude assistant: drive video creation in plain language from a conversation, which HeyGen doesn’t offer.
  • All-in-one suite built for advertising: the Generator (avatar + your voice + animated captions), Express (UGC via Veo), Motion Control (native 1080p), AI Images (visuals and product ads in 4K), AI Editing (an audio clip or raw video becomes an edited video), audio cleanup and a CapCut-style editor.
  • Motion Control: an image reproduces the motion of a reference video — a capability missing from HeyGen’s talking-head core.
  • Your real voice, no cloning: you record or upload your voice, with animated captions — a choice of authenticity, where HeyGen relies on cloning.
  • No commitment, payment via Whop: Starter €29.99, Pro €49.99, Elite from €89.99, with −25% on yearly billing.
  • Built in France by IA Manager, a French company.

On the other hand, both tools are in the same entry price range (~€29 vs ~$29): price isn’t the deciding factor. What sets them apart is the Claude connector and the all-in-one advertising focus.

Who is it for?

  • Choose HeyGen if your priority is multilingual lip-sync dubbing (175+ languages), voice cloning, a very large avatar library, and corporate or training video at an international scale, with an enterprise ecosystem (API, collaboration).
  • Choose AvatarAds if you’re a creator, e-commerce seller, small business or agency who wants to produce ads and UGC for TikTok, Instagram/Meta and YouTube without showing your face, use your real voice and drive creation from Claude.

Frequently asked questions

HeyGen or AvatarAds: which one should I choose?
It depends on what you need. HeyGen is the better fit if you want lip-sync dubbing in 175+ languages, voice cloning and a very large avatar library for corporate or training video at an international scale. AvatarAds is the better fit if you produce ads and UGC with your own voice (no cloning), want an MCP connector for Claude and an all-in-one suite built for advertising.
Is AvatarAds cheaper than HeyGen?
Both sit in the same price range: AvatarAds Starter is €29.99/month and HeyGen Creator is $29/month ($24 billed yearly). So price isn’t a decisive advantage. The real difference lies elsewhere: your real voice, the Claude connector and the all-in-one ad suite at AvatarAds; avatar realism, multilingual dubbing and voice cloning at HeyGen.
Which languages does HeyGen support?
HeyGen generates and dubs videos (voice and lip-sync) in 175+ languages. Its app itself is mainly in English.
Does AvatarAds clone voices like HeyGen?
No, and that’s a deliberate choice. AvatarAds uses your real voice, recorded or uploaded, with animated captions, for an authentic result. HeyGen offers native voice cloning: a real asset if that’s precisely the feature you’re looking for, but a different approach from AvatarAds’.
