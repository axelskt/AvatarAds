What is an AI-generated UGC video?

An AI UGC (user-generated content) video mimics the style of a real creator: smartphone framing, a natural tone, a direct hook. The difference is that nobody is filmed: an AI avatar or an image plays the character, and the voice comes from you or is generated. The result: credible creatives, produced at scale, for less.

How to create a UGC video with AvatarAds

  • Express (Veo engine): describe the scene in text or drop in an image, choose a UGC style, and generate the vertical video.
  • Generator: add an AI avatar that speaks with your real voice (recorded or uploaded) + animated captions — no voice cloning.
  • Motion Control: animate a photo (product, character) based on the motion of a reference video.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to show my face to make AI UGC?
No. AvatarAds is designed for “faceless” content: you use an AI avatar or an image, and you can add your own voice. Your face never appears unless you want it to.
Does AvatarAds’ AI UGC clone my voice?
No, and that’s a deliberate choice. The Generator uses your real voice, recorded or uploaded, combined with animated captions, for an authentic result with none of the risks of voice cloning. Express can also produce a generated voice if you prefer.
How long does it take to get a UGC video?
A few minutes. You type a script or drop in an image, choose the style, and the Veo engine generates the video. You can create several variants in a row to test different hooks.
