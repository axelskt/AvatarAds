Limits

Entry price: Starter $110/month (10 videos), Creator $220, Pro $550 / custom. Compare AvatarAds vs Arcads

No. 5 · E-commerce ads from a link

5. Creatify

Ideal for: generating e-commerce video ads from a simple product link, with the largest avatar library in this comparison.

  • 1500+ AI avatars, avatar AND voice cloning, 75+ languages, Batch Mode to multiply variants.
  • A rare “performance” layer: competitor ad tracking and performance analysis, plus a public API.

Limits

  • Credits don’t roll over from one month to the next (lost at the end of the month).
  • Scope centred on e-commerce ads; no official Claude MCP connector (only a third-party community wrapper).

Entry price: free (10 credits), then Starter $39/month (100 credits), Pro $99, Enterprise on quote. Compare AvatarAds vs Creatify
