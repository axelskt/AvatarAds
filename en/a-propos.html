About

AvatarAds, the French platform for AI ad videos

AvatarAds is a French SaaS platform for creating ad videos with artificial intelligence, published by IA Manager. It lets you produce ads and UGC content without ever showing your face.

AvatarAds in numbers

  • Pricing: Starter €29.99/month, Pro €49.99, Elite from €89.99 — −25% with yearly billing, no commitment.
  • 6 modules: Generator, Express, Motion Control, AI Images, AI Editing, Audio Cleanup + editor.
  • Publisher: IA Manager, a French company.
  • Formats: 9:16 vertical video for TikTok, Reels, Shorts and Meta; product images up to 4K.
  • Integration: official MCP connector for the Claude assistant.
  • Voice: your real voice, recorded or uploaded — no voice cloning.

AvatarAds in one sentence

AvatarAds brings together a suite of AI tools — talking AI avatars, UGC content, product images and ads, and automatic editing — to turn a text, an audio clip or an image into a vertical video ready to post on TikTok, Instagram (Reels), YouTube (Shorts) and Meta in minutes, with no camera and no editing skills.

The tools included

GeneratorAn AI avatar speaks with YOUR voice (recorded or uploaded) + animated captions — no voice cloning.
ExpressA realistic UGC video generated from a text or an image (Veo engine).
Motion ControlAn image reproduces the motion of a reference video (Motion 3.0, native 1080p).
AI ImagesVisuals and product ads in 4K from a simple prompt.
AI EditingYour audio or raw video becomes an edited video (captions, b-roll, music).
Audio Cleanup & EditorNoise, volume and silences cleaned up automatically · CapCut-style editing in your browser.

Who is it for?

Creators, e-commerce sellers, dropshippers, coaches, info-product creators, small businesses and agencies who need to test and publish lots of video creatives regularly, without filming themselves.
