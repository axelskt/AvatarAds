The ways to create a video with AI on AvatarAds

AvatarAds isn’t limited to one type of video: depending on your starting point (an idea, a text, an image or a recording), you pick the right module. Here are the seven ways to produce a video in the interface:

  • Generator: an AI avatar speaks with your own voice, recorded or uploaded, with synced animated captions — no voice cloning.
  • Express (Veo engine): from a simple text or an image, you get a creator-style UGC video, ready to publish in vertical format.
  • Motion Control: an image reproduces the motion of a reference video — ideal for animating a product photo or a character.
  • AI Editing: an audio clip or a raw video is automatically turned into an edited video (cuts, captions, pacing), with no manual work.
  • AI Images: generate visuals and product ads in 4K, to use in your videos or to animate with Motion Control.
  • Built-in CapCut-style editor: adjust, trim and style your videos by hand when you want to keep control of the result.
  • MCP connector for Claude: drive the whole creation process in plain language from a conversation with the Claude assistant.

Generator: an AI avatar that speaks with YOUR voice

The Generator creates a virtual presenter that speaks for you. Rather than a voice clone, it uses your real voice — recorded directly or uploaded — paired with synced animated captions. It’s a choice of authenticity: you stay “faceless” while keeping your real voice, with none of the risks of cloning.

Express: from a text or an image to a UGC video (Veo)

Express relies on the Veo engine to turn a written script or an uploaded image into a vertical UGC video, creator-content style. You describe the scene, choose a style, and the video is generated in minutes, ready for TikTok, Reels or Shorts.

Motion Control: animate an image with the motion of a video

Motion Control applies the motion of a reference video to a still image. You start from a photo — a product, a character or a visual — and you get a consistent animated video, with no shoot.

AI Editing: turn an audio clip or a raw video into an edited video

AI Editing takes an audio recording or a raw video and edits it automatically: cuts, captions, pacing and styling. It’s the fastest way to go from unedited raw material to a publishable video, with no editing skills.

AI Images and 4K product ads

The AI Images module generates visuals and product ads in 4K. These images serve as illustrations in your videos, as static creatives for e-commerce, or as a starting point to animate with Motion Control.

Built-in CapCut-style editor and audio cleanup

When you want to control the final result, the built-in CapCut-style editor lets you adjust cuts, text and styling. An audio cleanup module isolates the voice and improves the sound quality of your recordings before editing.

Drive creation from Claude (MCP connector)

AvatarAds has an official MCP connector for the Claude assistant. You generate and drive your videos in plain language, directly from a conversation — something the major competitors don’t offer. See how to connect Claude.
