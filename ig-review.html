Step 1 · Instagram Business Login

Connect your Instagram professional account

AvatarAds uses Instagram Business Login. You will be asked to grant these permissions:

instagram_business_basic read your username, profile picture, followers and posts

instagram_business_manage_insights show your account insights (28-day reach)

instagram_business_manage_comments read, post, reply to, hide and delete comments on your posts

instagram_business_manage_messages send direct messages to people who commented or messaged you

  • An Instagram window opens — log in to your professional account.
  • Review and allow the permissions listed above.
  • Come back to this page — your profile, insights, comments and messages appear below.
