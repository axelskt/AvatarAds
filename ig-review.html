Your posts

Select a post Open on Instagram

Post comment publishes the text as a new comment on the selected post, from your account.

Reply posts a public reply under the comment.
Hide / Unhide updates the comment's visibility. A hidden comment is only visible to you and its author.
Delete permanently removes the comment from Instagram.
Message sends a private reply to the comment's author — it arrives in their Instagram inbox. Our Auto-DM does this automatically when someone comments a keyword such as “GO”.
